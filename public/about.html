About This Site

This free loan calculator suite was built to give everyday people accurate, transparent financial calculations — without hidden agendas, upsells, or misleading numbers.

We cover over 20 loan types including conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, jumbo, ARM, HELOC, home equity loans, cash-out refinance, DSCR investment loans, hard money, bridge loans, and more.

Every calculator uses standard financial formulas including full amortization, CLTV, DSCR, effective APR, and DTI qualification limits sourced directly from Fannie Mae, HUD, the VA Lenders Handbook, and USDA guidelines.

Our Commitment

We do not receive compensation from any lender for favorable placement or recommendations. Results are estimates for educational purposes only. Always verify with a licensed financial professional before making any borrowing decision.
