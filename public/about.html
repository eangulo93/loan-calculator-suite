Our Mission

Most people walk into a lender's office without knowing their numbers. They don't know their LTV, their DTI, their maximum borrowing amount, or what their payment will actually be after taxes and insurance. Lenders know all of this — borrowers often don't. That information gap costs people money.

My Loan Breakdown was built to close that gap. Our free calculator suite covers over 20 loan types with the same formulas, guidelines, and rate data that licensed mortgage professionals use. We pull live rates directly from Freddie Mac every week. We source our DTI limits from official Fannie Mae, HUD, VA, and USDA guidelines — not estimates.

When you talk to a lender, you should already know your numbers. We make that possible — for free, with no account required.

🎖️

Veteran-Run. Built on Service.

My Loan Breakdown is proudly veteran-owned and operated. The same values that define military service — precision, integrity, and putting others first — define how we build our tools and present information. No misleading numbers. No hidden agendas. Just accurate data and transparent calculations.

25+ Years of Mortgage Experience

The team behind My Loan Breakdown brings over 25 years of hands-on mortgage industry experience — from origination and underwriting to investment lending and home equity products. We've seen every loan type, worked with every borrower profile, and understand what actually matters when qualifying for a loan.

That experience is what makes our calculators accurate. We don't build formulas from textbooks — we build them from real-world lending experience. When we say a DSCR of 1.25x qualifies at most lenders for best-rate pricing, that's not a guess. It's what we've seen in practice across hundreds of transactions.

Disclosure

All calculators on My Loan Breakdown provide estimates for educational and informational purposes only. Results do not constitute financial, legal, tax, or lending advice. Actual loan terms, rates, fees, and eligibility are determined by lenders based on individual financial circumstances.

Market rates are sourced from Freddie Mac PMMS via the Federal Reserve FRED database and updated weekly. DTI qualification limits are sourced from Fannie Mae SEL guidelines, HUD Handbook 4000.1, VA Lenders Handbook, and USDA HB-1-3555.
